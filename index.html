The platform

A social platform connecting local cycling, motorcycling, and driving enthusiasts.

Discover and connect with local riders and drivers who share your obsession. MassHub brings the people behind the passion together — organize group rides, find events near you, and build real community around the machines you love. No noise, no gatekeeping. Just enthusiasts, connected.

  • Discover local enthusiasts
  • Find group rides & events
  • Build real connections

Pick your lane

Your feed stays yours.

Cyclists see cycling. Riders see riding. Drivers see driving. Choose one lane or all three — nothing bleeds over unless you want it to.

Cyclists

Road, gravel, MTB, fixed. Find the Saturday group ride that matches your pace.

  • Group rides filtered by pace & distance
  • Local routes shared by riders
  • Shop rides & club meetups

Motorcyclists

Sport, cruiser, ADV, vintage. Link up for canyon runs, bike nights and long hauls.

  • Rides grouped by style & experience
  • Bike nights & local meets
  • Riders near you, not across the country

Drivers

JDM, euro, muscle, track. Cars & coffee, cruises, and the people who wrench.

  • Meets & cruises near you
  • Build logs from local owners
  • Track days & drive events
